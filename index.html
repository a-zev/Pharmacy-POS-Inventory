Pharmacy POS & Inventory — Feature Roadmap

Production-oriented checklist from critical pharmacy operations to advanced automation. Select modules/features to mark them as planned, completed, or included in the implementation scope.

P0 — CRITICAL

Core POS & Pharmacy Operations

1. User & Access Management
Secure accounts, roles, permissions, and access control.

Controls who can access the system and what each employee is allowed to see or perform. Sensitive actions such as voids, refunds, price changes, prescription dispensing, and inventory adjustments should require appropriate permissions.

Features

  • User accounts, login/logout, password management, activation/deactivation, session control.
  • Roles such as Administrator, Pharmacist, Pharmacy Assistant, Cashier, Inventory Staff, Purchasing Staff, Manager, and Auditor.
  • Granular permissions per module and action: view, add, edit, approve, void, refund, adjust, export, and manage.
  • Login history, failed-login tracking, session monitoring, and security controls.

2. Product & Medicine Master
Central catalog for medicines, OTC products, devices, and other merchandise.

Provides the authoritative product definition used by POS, inventory, purchasing, pricing, reporting, and prescription workflows.

Features

  • SKU, barcode, generic name, brand name, description, dosage form, strength, unit, manufacturer, supplier, category, subcategory, and status.
  • Medicine classifications such as OTC, prescription, antibiotic, maintenance, generic, branded, supplement, medical device, and non-medicine.
  • Configurable product status, reorder settings, tax settings, and product notes.
  • Duplicate detection and controlled editing of master data.
3. Barcode Management
Fast and reliable barcode identification throughout the pharmacy.

Associates manufacturer and internal barcodes with products so staff can scan products at POS, receiving, stock counting, and transfers.

Features

  • Multiple barcodes per product, internal barcodes, manufacturer barcodes, barcode validation, and duplicate detection.
  • Barcode lookup optimized for keyboard scanners and USB/Bluetooth scanners.
  • Fallback product search by SKU, generic name, or brand name.
4. Pharmacy POS / Sales
Primary point-of-sale workflow for retail and pharmacy transactions.

Provides the cashier-facing sales interface for scanning products, building a cart, applying authorized discounts, selecting customers, processing payments, and completing transactions.

Features

  • Barcode scanning, product search, quantity controls, cart management, held/recall transactions, customer selection, discounts, and payment processing.
  • Automatic stock availability checking and batch/FEFO allocation where applicable.
  • Prescription-required product warnings and pharmacist verification workflow.
  • Transaction confirmation, receipt generation, and controlled reprinting.
5. Payment Management
Record and reconcile all payment methods used at the register.

Supports cash and electronic payments while preserving payment references and reconciliation data.

Features

  • Cash, GCash, Maya, debit, credit card, bank transfer, QR, and configurable payment types.
  • Reference number capture for non-cash payments.
  • Split/multiple payment support can be enabled if required.
  • Payment method reporting and cashier reconciliation.
6. Receipt & Printing
Generate compliant-looking transaction receipts and support thermal printers.

Produces customer receipts and internal copies from finalized transactions, with support for common thermal receipt formats.

Features

  • 58mm and 80mm receipt layouts.
  • Pharmacy identity, transaction number, date/time, products, quantities, discounts, tax, total, payment, change, and cashier.
  • Receipt preview, controlled reprint, and printer configuration.
7. Sales Transactions
Permanent transaction records linking sales, products, batches, customers, and payments.

Stores finalized sales as structured records rather than editable/deletable documents. Each sale should retain its items, quantities, prices, discounts, batch allocation, cashier, payment, and timestamps.

Features

  • Transaction header and line-item records.
  • Batch-level item allocation for traceability.
  • Search, filtering, viewing, printing, and export.
  • Immutable transaction history with reversal/void mechanisms instead of deletion.
8. Void / Cancellation
Controlled reversal of incorrect or invalid transactions.

Prevents users from silently deleting completed sales. A void should require a reason and, for sensitive cases, authorization by a supervisor or manager.

Features

  • Void reason, authorization, reversal of inventory, reversal of payment where applicable, and complete audit trail.
  • Configurable time limits and approval requirements.
  • Void reporting by cashier, user, date, and reason.
9. Sales Returns & Refunds
Manage customer returns without corrupting sales or inventory history.

Links returned items to the original sale where possible and records the reason, quantity, refund, and inventory disposition.

Features

  • Original transaction lookup, partial item returns, return quantities, reason, refund method, and authorization.
  • Disposition options: restock, damaged, quarantine, expired, or supplier return.
  • Automatic inventory and financial reversal records.
10. Cash Register & Shift
Opening, operating, and closing controls for each POS register.

Creates a clear financial boundary around each cashier shift so expected cash can be compared with actual cash at closing.

Features

  • Opening cash, register assignment, cashier assignment, shift start/end.
  • Cash sales, refunds, cash-in, cash-out, expenses, and other register movements.
  • Expected cash versus actual cash, variance, and closing report.
  • Supervisor approval for closing or significant variances.
11. Audit Trail
Tamper-resistant history of important system and business actions.

Records who changed important information, what changed, when it changed, and why. This is essential for accountability in pharmacy, POS, and inventory operations.

Features

  • User, action, module, record ID, timestamp, and source information.
  • Old value/new value tracking for sensitive edits.
  • Audit events for price changes, stock adjustments, voids, refunds, permissions, prescriptions, and master-data changes.
  • Searchable audit reports with restricted access.
P0 — CRITICAL

Inventory Control

12. Inventory Management
Central stock control for every product and location.

Tracks stock quantities and statuses across the pharmacy. Inventory should be driven by transactions rather than manually overwriting a single stock number.

Features

  • On-hand, available, reserved, damaged, expired, quarantine, and in-transit quantities.
  • Stock movement history for purchases, sales, returns, transfers, adjustments, damage, and expiration.
  • Location-aware inventory for branches, shelves, warehouses, and registers where required.
13. Batch / Lot Tracking
Track medicines at batch level for expiry, recall, receiving, and traceability.

Every received batch can carry its own lot number, expiration date, cost, selling price, supplier, and quantity. This enables precise identification of stock affected by expiration or recall.

Features

  • Batch number, product, manufacturing date, expiration date, quantity, purchase cost, selling price, supplier, and receiving date.
  • Batch history from receiving through sale, return, adjustment, or disposal.
  • Batch lookup and traceability reporting.
14. Expiration Management
Prevent expired products from being sold and identify upcoming expiry risks.

Automatically identifies expired and soon-to-expire batches using configurable warning periods.

Features

  • Expired, expiring today, ≤30 days, ≤60 days, ≤90 days, and good-stock classifications.
  • Dashboard alerts and expiry reports.
  • Configurable POS blocking rules for expired batches.
  • Quarantine/disposal workflow for expired inventory.
15. FEFO Stock Allocation
First Expired, First Out batch allocation for safer stock rotation.

When multiple batches are available, the system preferentially allocates the batch with the earliest valid expiration date, subject to pharmacy policy and operational rules.

Features

  • Automatic batch selection during sales and dispensing.
  • Manual override only with appropriate permission.
  • Batch allocation visibility in POS and transaction history.
16. Stock Adjustment
Controlled correction of physical inventory differences.

Provides a formal process for changes caused by damage, loss, found stock, counting errors, internal use, or approved data correction.

Features

  • Quantity, batch, reason, remarks, user, timestamp, and approval.
  • Positive and negative adjustments.
  • Adjustment history and variance reporting.
17. Physical Stock Count
Reconcile system quantities with actual physical inventory.

Supports periodic physical inventory counts without directly manipulating stock balances.

Features

  • Full stock count, category count, cycle count, random count, and barcode-based counting.
  • System quantity versus physical quantity and variance.
  • Investigation and approval before posting adjustments.
18. Low Stock & Reorder Levels
Identify products approaching stockout and prepare replenishment recommendations.

Uses minimum stock, reorder point, maximum stock, and preferred order quantity to identify replenishment needs.

Features

  • Per-product and per-branch reorder configuration.
  • Low-stock dashboard, alerts, and purchase suggestions.
  • Optional supplier preference and lead-time data.
P1 — HIGH

Purchasing & Supplier Management

19. Supplier Management
Maintain supplier information, contacts, terms, and purchasing history.

Stores supplier master data and connects suppliers with products, purchase orders, receiving, returns, and purchasing reports.

Features

  • Company name, contact person, phone, email, address, tax information, payment terms, and status.
  • Supplier product catalog and purchase history.
  • Supplier performance information where useful.
20. Purchase Orders
Formalize purchasing from request through supplier order.

Creates controlled purchase requests and purchase orders with approval and receiving status.

Features

  • PO number, supplier, order date, expected delivery, products, quantities, costs, discounts, taxes, and total.
  • Status flow: Draft, Pending Approval, Approved, Ordered, Partially Received, Received, Cancelled.
  • Purchase history and printable/exportable PO documents.
21. Purchase Receiving
Receive actual quantities and batches instead of assuming the PO was fulfilled exactly.

Captures what physically arrived from the supplier, including actual batch and expiry details. This is the point where inventory is added to stock.

Features

  • Ordered versus received quantity comparison.
  • Batch number, expiry date, quantity, unit cost, selling price, damage, and accepted quantity.
  • Partial receiving and multiple receipts against one PO.
  • Automatic creation of inventory ledger entries.
22. Supplier Returns
Return damaged, incorrect, recalled, or near-expiry products to suppliers.

Creates a traceable supplier return document linked to inventory batches and the original receiving transaction.

Features

  • Product, batch, quantity, reason, supplier, date, and status.
  • Reasons such as damaged, near expiry, wrong product, wrong quantity, recall, and other configured reasons.
  • Automatic stock deduction and supplier-return reporting.
P1 — HIGH

Pharmacy-Specific Operations

23. Customer Management
Maintain customer identity and transaction information with appropriate access controls.

Provides customer records for receipts, purchase history, loyalty, discounts, and pharmacy workflows. Sensitive medical information should have stronger permissions than ordinary customer data.

Features

  • Customer ID, name, contact, address, customer type, discount eligibility, and tax information.
  • Purchase history, returns, invoices, and configurable customer notes.
  • Privacy-aware access control and audit logging.
24. Prescription Management
Record and verify prescriptions before dispensing prescription-required medicines.

Supports a controlled workflow where prescription-required products can be reviewed by an authorized pharmacist before completion.

Features

  • Prescription number, prescriber, relevant license/reference information, patient, issue date, medicine, dosage, quantity, and refill information as applicable.
  • Prescription attachment or image where appropriate.
  • Verification status, pharmacist identity, dispensing record, and audit history.
  • Configurable rules for products requiring prescription verification.
25. Pharmacist Verification & Dispensing
Separate pharmacist review from ordinary cashier operations.

Provides an approval step for transactions that require professional verification. The system should make it clear who verified and what was ultimately dispensed.

Features

  • Pending verification queue.
  • Product, quantity, prescription, customer, and relevant transaction information.
  • Approve, reject, modify, or require clarification according to configured workflow.
  • Pharmacist identity and timestamp recorded in the transaction.
26. Generic / Brand Relationships
Connect equivalent product records for search and pharmacy workflows.

Links products by generic composition, brand, strength, dosage form, or other configured product attributes so staff can locate appropriate available products.

Features

  • Generic group, brand relationships, strength, dosage form, and manufacturer.
  • Search by generic or brand.
  • Configurable substitution/dispensing workflow subject to professional and legal requirements.
P2 — MEDIUM

Pricing, Promotions & Business Controls

27. Price Management
Manage cost, retail, wholesale, member, and promotional pricing with history.

Centralizes pricing rules and preserves historical price changes so the business can understand when and why prices changed.

Features

  • Cost price, retail price, wholesale price, member price, and promotional price.
  • Effective dates and price history.
  • Approval workflow for sensitive price changes.
  • Margin and markup visibility.
28. Discounts & Promotions
Configure controlled discounts and promotional pricing.

Supports common pharmacy retail promotions while preventing unauthorized discounting.

Features

  • Buy-one-get-one, quantity discounts, member discounts, category discounts, bundles, and date-limited promotions.
  • Per-line or transaction-level discounts.
  • Authorization thresholds and discount audit logs.
29. Inventory Valuation & Profitability
Measure stock value, revenue, cost of goods sold, and gross profit.

Transforms transaction and purchasing data into business-level profitability information.

Features

  • Revenue, COGS, gross profit, gross margin, stock valuation, and product-level profitability.
  • Configurable costing methodology appropriate to the business's accounting practice.
  • Profitability by product, category, branch, cashier, and period.
30. Customer Loyalty
Optional membership, points, rewards, and customer incentives.

Creates a configurable loyalty program without coupling it to the core inventory system.

Features

  • Membership levels, points earning, redemption, rewards, and member-specific discounts.
  • Customer transaction history and loyalty activity.
  • Controls for excluded products or regulated transaction types where required.
P2 — MEDIUM

Reports & Dashboards

31. Sales Reports
Analyze transaction volume, revenue, payment methods, cashiers, and adjustments.

Provides operational and management reporting for daily sales and historical performance.

Features

  • Daily, monthly, annual, hourly, product, category, cashier, branch, and payment-method reports.
  • Transaction, void, refund, discount, and cancellation reports.
  • Filtering, date ranges, grouping, print, and export.
32. Inventory Reports
Understand stock levels, movement, expiry, valuation, and discrepancies.

Provides detailed visibility into inventory health and stock-control performance.

Features

  • Current stock, stock movement, batch inventory, expiry, expired products, low stock, dead stock, fast/slow movers, adjustments, and valuation.
  • Batch-level traceability and stock reconciliation reports.
33. Purchasing Reports
Analyze purchasing costs, suppliers, purchase orders, receiving, and returns.

Helps purchasing staff and management review supplier activity and procurement performance.

Features

  • Purchase history, supplier purchases, PO status, receiving, supplier returns, purchase costs, and product procurement history.
  • Supplier comparisons and period-based purchasing analysis.
34. Management Dashboard
Single operational view of sales, inventory, purchasing, expiry, and exceptions.

Displays actionable KPIs rather than only raw data.

Features

  • Today's sales, transactions, gross profit, low-stock items, expiring batches, expired stock, pending POs, returns, voids, and cash variance.
  • Role-specific dashboard widgets.
P2 — MEDIUM

Multi-Branch & Warehouse

35. Branch Management
Operate multiple pharmacy branches from one centralized system.

Separates branch-level inventory, registers, users, and sales while maintaining a central product and management structure.

Features

  • Branches, registers, branch users, branch-specific stock, and consolidated reporting.
  • Central product master and configurable branch pricing.
36. Stock Transfer
Move inventory between branches, warehouse, and other authorized locations.

Creates a formal transfer lifecycle so inventory remains accountable while moving between locations.

Features

  • Transfer request, approval, dispatch, in-transit, receiving, and completion.
  • Product and batch-level quantities.
  • Transfer discrepancies and audit history.
37. Warehouse Management
Manage centralized stock separately from retail branch inventory.

Useful when purchasing is centralized and stock is distributed to several branches.

Features

  • Warehouse locations, receiving, picking, transfers, stock counts, and batch tracking.
  • Branch replenishment and warehouse-level reorder management.
P3 — LOW

Operational Enhancements

38. Offline POS
Continue limited POS operations during temporary connectivity failures.

Allows a register to queue eligible transactions locally and synchronize when connectivity returns. This requires careful conflict, duplicate, security, and regulated-product handling.

Features

  • Local transaction queue, synchronization, retry handling, duplicate protection, and conflict resolution.
  • Explicit restrictions for workflows that must remain online or require centralized verification.
39. Barcode & Shelf Label Printing
Generate internal labels, shelf labels, price tags, and barcode labels.

Reduces manual labeling work and improves scanning accuracy in the pharmacy.

Features

  • Product name, barcode, SKU, price, and configurable label fields.
  • Batch/expiry labels where appropriate.
  • Common label printer and paper-size support.
40. Notifications & Alerts
Deliver operational warnings to responsible staff.

Centralizes alerts for exceptions that require action rather than relying solely on dashboards.

Features

  • Low stock, expiry, expired stock, pending approvals, pending POs, supplier returns, cash variances, and other configurable events.
  • In-app notifications with optional email/SMS/messaging integration.
41. Inventory Aging & Dead Stock
Identify capital tied up in slow-moving or non-moving inventory.

Analyzes stock by time since receipt or movement to support purchasing and stock-reduction decisions.

Features

  • 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91–180, and 180+ day aging bands.
  • Dead stock and slow-moving product reports.
  • Branch and category comparisons.
42. ABC Inventory Analysis
Prioritize inventory management using financial or movement-based classifications.

Groups products into configurable A/B/C classes based on selected business metrics such as annual consumption value or sales contribution.

Features

  • Configurable classification thresholds.
  • Reports for purchasing, cycle counting, and management review.
P4 — FUTURE

Integrations, Automation & Advanced Intelligence

43. Automatic Reorder Recommendations
Recommend purchase quantities from stock levels, sales velocity, and supplier lead time.

Calculates suggested replenishment rather than automatically placing orders. This keeps purchasing decisions under staff control.

Features

  • Average daily sales, reorder point, safety stock, lead time, current stock, outstanding PO quantities, and recommended order quantity.
  • Supplier selection and purchase-order draft generation.
44. Demand Forecasting
Estimate future product demand using historical sales and operational factors.

Uses historical transaction patterns to estimate future demand and possible stockout dates. Forecasts should be presented as recommendations with transparent assumptions.

Features

  • Sales history, seasonality, trends, lead time, safety stock, forecast quantity, and projected stockout date.
  • Forecast accuracy monitoring.
45. Payment Gateway Integration
Connect electronic payments directly to transaction records.

Integrates supported payment providers or terminals so payment references and transaction status can be captured automatically.

Features

  • QR/e-wallet, card terminal, bank, and other supported payment methods.
  • Payment confirmation, reference IDs, reconciliation, retries, and failure handling.
46. Accounting Integration
Synchronize sales, purchases, payments, COGS, and other accounting data.

Reduces duplicate data entry between POS/inventory and accounting systems.

Features

  • Sales summaries, purchase data, payment settlements, inventory valuation, COGS, and configurable accounting mappings.
  • Export/API integration and synchronization logs.
47. Online Pharmacy / E-Commerce
Extend the product catalog and order workflow to online channels.

Provides an online ordering layer connected to centralized product availability and pharmacy fulfillment workflows.

Features

  • Online catalog, cart, customer accounts, orders, pickup/delivery, inventory reservation, and fulfillment.
  • Prescription upload and pharmacist verification for applicable products.
  • Order status synchronization with POS.
48. AI Search & Reporting
Natural-language access to product discovery and business reporting.

Allows authorized users to ask questions such as which products are low in stock or which batches expire within a selected period. AI should operate over controlled system data and respect user permissions.

Features

  • Natural-language product search.
  • Natural-language report queries.
  • Automated summaries and anomaly detection.
  • Permission-aware access and explicit source data behind generated answers.
49. Drug Information / Interaction Reference
Optional clinical-reference integration for authorized pharmacy staff.

Can connect to an appropriate drug-information source for reference information and interaction checking. This should be treated as decision support and not a replacement for professional pharmacist judgment.

Features

  • Drug reference lookup, warnings, interaction information, and configurable reference links.
  • Source/version tracking where supported by the provider.
